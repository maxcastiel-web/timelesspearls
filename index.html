Pearl care
  • Put pearls on after perfume, makeup and lotion; take them off first.
  • Wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth. No ultrasonic or harsh cleaners.
  • Store in the gift box or a soft pouch, away from harder jewelry.
  • Remove before swimming, showering, exercise and sleep.
  • Keep away from long sun, saunas and high heat.

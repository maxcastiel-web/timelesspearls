Pearl Care

Made to last for generations

Pearls are organic gems. A little care keeps their glow for decades.

01

Last on, first off

Put pearls on after perfume, hairspray, makeup and lotion — and take them off first.

02

Wear them often

A little natural skin moisture helps keep their luster alive.

03

Wipe gently

Use a soft, lint-free cloth, lightly dampened if needed. No ultrasonic or harsh cleaners.

04

Store apart

Keep them in their gift box or a soft pouch, away from harder jewelry.

05

Avoid water

Remove before swimming, hot tubs or long showers — chlorine and soap dull the surface.

06

Keep them cool

Protect from long sun, saunas and high heat, which can dry pearls out.
